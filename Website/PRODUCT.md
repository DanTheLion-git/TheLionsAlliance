## Platform

web

## Users

A mixed audience, with contact as the shared action:

- **Primary:** staff and volunteers of smaller, independent museums and heritage venues (often in Limburg / the Peel region). They read on a laptop or phone, usually outside opening hours, often to prepare a board conversation. They want to see that the thing exists and works in a museum like theirs.
- **Also:** municipalities and tourism partners (Immersive Worlds, walking routes), and event clients who arrive via WeddingCamBox / ShutterShare.

## Product Purpose

The Lions Alliance is a two-person creative experience studio (Daniel and Thirza van Leeuwen) that builds the physical and digital objects that let a venue's own story be touched, answered and walked: touch tables, a talking portrait, walking quests with a hand-built key, and 360° VR. Success is a visitor contacting the studio.

## Positioning

Everything is built for one specific place and its archive, by the same two people who design, write, build and install it. The proof is physical and local: the Medelotapps touch table and the Jan Truijen portrait running at Museum Medelo in Meijel, a real brass Skeleton Key, and VRVoyage live in Amsterdam.
