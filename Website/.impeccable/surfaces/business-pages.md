# Surface brief: business pages

Targets: src/pages/index.astro, touchscreens/, jan-truijen/, puzzlequests/, immersive/, contact/ (+ every /nl/ mirror).
Mode: Persuade. Mixed audience (museums first; municipalities/tourism; event clients). Shared action: contact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio's site is an exhibition hall: every product is hung as an exhibit, photographed in situ, with a plain wall label. It refuses the agency template (centred hero, overline, card grid, values grid, stat strip) and the cream-serif-gold heritage default it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Plaster-grey wall ground (#E7E6E1), ink-blue panel bands from the logo's own field (#16222B) owning whole sections, one signal-yellow accent (#F3C623) for actions only. Schibsted Grotesk for everything; weight and size carry hierarchy. Square corners (2px). Wall labels: hairline rule, title, one-line description, facts as short label/value lines. No inventory numbers, no archive codes (user rejected), no kickers, no section numbers.

STORY: A museum volunteer sees a real table, portrait, key and VR boat working in real places, understands that two people build each one for one venue, and gets in touch. Event and tourism visitors find their door without detours.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: plain headline saying what the studio builds and for whom, one sentence, yellow "Get in touch" plus a text link to the work. Right ~58%: one exhibit photo, large, with its wall label beneath and a "Next exhibit" control; cycles four real exhibits.

FORM: Exhibition hall, option 1 of 4 (hand-derived). seed: none (unavailable) — launcher blocked, directions presented through the structured tool; user locked "Exhibition hall" and rejected inventory-number styling.

PRODUCT PAGES (2026-11-24): touchscreens, jan-truijen, puzzlequests and immersive are catalogue entries (src/components/hall/CataloguePage.astro): Plate 1 sticky on the left, the entry (fact table, lede, sections) on the right, numbered plates and an ink enquiry block below. Chosen by the user from a surface roll, seed:755309a0a005e74e, card C of the dealt three.

Motion: focal "unveil" (clip-path wipe off photos, label lines mount); cross-document view transitions morph exhibit photos into their pages; feedback on buttons/links only; reduced motion = crossfades, no autoplay.
